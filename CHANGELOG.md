# Changelog

## [1.1.0] - 2026-11-25

- Major beginner-friendly pedagogical overhaul across all 18 chapters (Lectures 1–21):
  - Added reusable **Everyday Analogy**, step-by-step **Worked Example**, **Common Mistake (Pitfall)**, and **Try It Yourself / Guided Experiments** components across Theory, Playground, and Formulas & Proofs tabs.
  - Expanded every chapter to 3–5 progressive Theory sections with upgraded 2D/3D/matrix interactive demos, clickable preset scenarios, and plain-language symbol introductions.
  - Upgraded every Playground with one-click preset buttons and 3–4 step-by-step guided discovery experiments.
  - Expanded Formulas & Proofs with multiple step-by-step interactive derivations, worked examples, reference tables, and exam tips.
  - Expanded the verified bilingual practice bank to **128 questions** (7–8 questions per chapter) with misconception-specific explanations and computed checks.
  - Expanded the interactive Math Glossary to **62 beginner-friendly terms**.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-25

- Initial release of the interactive University Mathematics Learning Platform covering all 21 lectures (18 chapters):
  - **Part I — Foundations & Geometry:** Functions & Transcendental Functions (L1), Numbers, Points & Vectors (L2), Plane Geometry (L3+4), Space Geometry (L5).
  - **Part II — Linear Algebra:** Matrices & Vectors (L6), Square Matrices (L7), Linear Systems (L8), Vector Spaces (L9+10), Basis, Dimension & Coordinates (L11), Inner Product Spaces & Best Approximation (L12).
  - **Part III — Differential Calculus:** Limits (L13), Derivatives & Applications of Derivatives (L14+15).
  - **Part IV — Series & Integration:** Sequences & Series of Real Numbers (L16), Function Series (L17), Indefinite Integrals (L18), Definite Integrals (L19), Applications of Definite Integrals (L20).
  - **Part V — Complex Analysis:** Complex Numbers (L21).
- Every chapter includes Theory with live 2D/3D visualizations, an interactive Playground, step-by-step Formulas & Proofs, and verified Practice exercises.
- Full Turkish/English (`TR`/`EN`) bilingual switching, Light/Dark/System themes, searchable Glossary with inline popups, and offline `file://` support.
